V11 - MAX30102 bus stability
- Do not classify one transient FIFO read error as a missing sensor.
- Confirm address loss repeatedly before showing MAX30102 MISSING.
- Retry MAX30102 I2C register/FIFO transactions.
- Reconfigure stalled FIFO without declaring device removal.
- Give MAX30102 priority over RTC on I2C3.
- Delay/rate-limit RTC probing and retain software-clock fallback.
- Lower finger IR threshold to 5000 for weak breakout boards.
